## 1. Business Website

**Kya hai:** Business ki poori website — 1 se 5 pages (package ke hisaab se).

**Pages ho sakte hain:** Home, Services, About, Gallery, Contact.

**Kiske liye:** Shop, clinic, salon, gym, restaurant — jisko poori website chahiye.

**Included:** Mobile responsive, WhatsApp button, Google Maps, contact form, basic SEO, SSL.

## 2. Landing Page

**Kya hai:** Ek page, ek hi kaam ke liye banaya gaya — visitor ko samjhana aur enquiry / WhatsApp pe bhejna.

**Kiske liye:** Instagram / Facebook page wale seller aur creator. Bio link se aane wala visitor yahan aayega.

**Included:** Hero, services / products, price (agar customer de), photos, WhatsApp button, contact form.

## 3. Booking / Enquiry Website

**Kya hai:** Services + timing + appointment / enquiry form wali website. Customer form bharega, business ko enquiry milegi.

**Kiske liye:** Clinic, salon, tuition, coaching — jahan appointment ya admission enquiry hoti hai.

**Included (Premium package me):** Booking / appointment form, services + timing, WhatsApp button, Maps, basic SEO.

**Note:** Ye real-time slot booking nahi hai (jaise doctor ka live calendar). Ye enquiry form hai — customer time batata hai, business confirm karta hai. Live calendar system alag kaam hai, is service me nahi hai.

## 4. Catalogue / Menu Website

**Kya hai:** Products ya menu ki list — photo, naam, price — aur WhatsApp order button.

**Kiske liye:** Shop, restaurant, cafe, boutique, home baker.

**Included (Premium package me):** Catalogue / menu list, WhatsApp order button, Maps, contact form.

**Note:** Ye online payment wala store nahi hai. Customer WhatsApp pe order karega, payment business aur customer ke beech me hoga (UPI / cash — business decide karega).
