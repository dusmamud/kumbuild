# Kumbuild — Refund Policy

Status: **DRAFT (2026-11-09). Ye policy final nahi hai. Website pe daalne se pehle user ko approve karna hai.**

Ye policy `PRICING.md` aur `TERMS.md` se match karti hai. Koi bhi number badle to teenon files me badalna hai.

## 1. Basic rule — DRAFT

- **Kaam shuru hone se pehle cancel karo to poora advance refund hoga.** Kaam shuru ka matlab: Kumbuild ne website banana shuru kar diya ho.
- **Kaam shuru hone ke baad advance refund nahi hoga.** Advance isliye liya jata hai kyunki usi se kaam shuru hota hai.
- **Final payment (baaki 50%) website live karne se pehle liya jata hai.** Live hone ke baad final payment ka refund nahi hoga, kyunki website deliver ho chuki hai.

Ye rules draft hain — user ko approve karna hai ki advance kab se "kaam shuru" mana jayega.

## 2. Refund kab milega?

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| Order kiya, advance diya, kaam shuru nahi hua, cancel kiya | Poora advance refund |
| Kaam shuru ho gaya, customer cancel karta hai | Advance refund nahi hoga |
| Preview pasand nahi aaya, 2 revision rounds ke baad bhi approve nahi kiya | Case-by-case — Kumbuild aur customer baat karke decide karenge (ye line draft hai, final policy me saaf karna hai) |
| Kumbuild order deliver nahi kar paya (apni galti se) | Poora payment refund (advance + final, jo bhi liya ho) |
| Payment galti se double ho gaya | Extra payment refund hoga |
| Renewal fees pay kar di, renewal shuru nahi hua | Renewal fees refund hogi (agar renewal period shuru nahi hua ho) |

## 3. Refund kaise milega?

- Customer `home.kumbuild.com/support` se ya WhatsApp pe refund maangega
- Kumbuild 7 working days (draft) me request check karega aur batayega refund milega ya nahi, aur kitna milega
- Refund Razorpay se usi payment method pe milega jis se payment aaya tha
- Razorpay ki refund processing fee ₹0 hai (pricing page, May 2026). Original transaction pe kati platform fee ka treatment Razorpay dashboard / activation ke time confirm karna hai.

## 4. Refund nahi milega — in cases me

- Website live ho chuki hai aur customer ko bas pasand nahi aayi (design pasand na aana refund ki wajah nahi hai — iske liye revision rounds hain)
- Customer ne galat content diya aur usi se website galat bani
- Customer ne preview ka jawab hi nahi diya aur order `On hold` me chala gaya
- Renewal period shuru ho chuka hai

## 5. Order cancel kaise karein?

- Order cancel karne ke liye customer ko support pe message karna hoga — order ID ke saath
- Cancel karne ke baad order ka status dashboard pe dikhega
- Jo refund banta hoga, woh upar wale rules ke hisaab se milega
