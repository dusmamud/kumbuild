# Kumbuild — FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

Status: **DRAFT (2026-11-09).** Ye website ke `/faq` page ka base banega. Jawab `PLAN.md`, `PRICING.md`, `DELIVERY.md` aur `REFUND.md` se match karte hain.

## Website ke baare me

**Q: Kumbuild kya hai?**
A: Kumbuild ek done-for-you website service hai. Tum batao kya banana hai, Kumbuild website bana ke dega. Tumhe code nahi seekhna, kisi tool me design nahi karna.

**Q: Meri website kitne din me ban jayegi?**
A: 3–5 working days (draft) — advance payment aur poora content milne ke baad, preview link milne tak. Content late mile to time aage badhega.

**Q: Website banane ke liye mujhe kya dena hoga?**
A: Business ka naam, services aur price, WhatsApp number, address, timing, aur real photos. Poori list `CONTENT.md` me hai.

**Q: Kya mujhe khud kuch karna hoga?**
A: Sirf form bharna hai, content dena hai, preview dekh ke approve karna hai, aur payment karna hai. Banana Kumbuild ka kaam hai.

## Price aur payment

**Q: Website kitne ki banegi?**
A: Starter ₹3,999, Business ₹7,999, Premium ₹14,999 (sab draft hain). Har saal renewal ₹1,999 (draft) hai. Detail `PRICING.md` me hai.

**Q: Payment kaise karna hoga?**
A: 50% advance order ke time, baaki 50% approval ke baad, website live karne se pehle. Payment Razorpay se hoga — UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets chalenge.

**Q: Kya renewal har saal dena hoga?**
A: Haan, ₹1,999 / saal (draft) — domain + hosting + chhoti updates + support ke liye. Renewal na karne pe kya hoga, ye policy abhi final nahi hui hai.

## Revision aur changes

**Q: Website pasand na aaye to kya hoga?**
A: 2 revision rounds free hain. Preview dekh ke changes batao, Kumbuild karke dobara preview bhejega. Uske baad extra charge lagega — amount TBD.

**Q: Baad me website me kuch badalna ho to?**
A: Chhoti updates (phone, timing, ek-do lines) renewal me included hain. Bade changes (naya page, design badalna) alag charge pe honge.

## Account aur login

**Q: Kya mujhe account banana hoga?**
A: Haan — order karne ke liye `account.kumbuild.com` pe signup karo (naam + email + password). Uske baad tum apne orders `home.kumbuild.com` pe track kar sakte ho.

**Q: Login kaise karun?**
A: `account.kumbuild.com/login` pe email + password daalo. Login ke baad admin ko admin dashboard aur customer ko home dashboard (My orders) pe bheja jayega. Password bhool jao to "Forgot password" se reset link maango.

## Order aur delivery

**Q: Order kaise karun?**
A: WhatsApp DM karo, Instagram DM karo, ya `kumbuild.com/order` pe form bharo — package choose karo, business details do, advance do. Phir `account.kumbuild.com` pe signup/login karo — poora flow `PLAN.md` me hai.

**Q: Order ka status kahan dekhu?**
A: `home.kumbuild.com` pe login karo — My orders me har order ka status dikhega: Received → In progress → Preview ready → Revision → Approved → Live. Status admin tumhe WhatsApp pe bhi batayega. Preview link aur live link dono jagah milenge.

**Q: Meri website ka domain kya hoga?**
A: Domain naam tum choose karoge. Pehle saal ka domain package price me mana gaya hai (draft — final karte time saaf hoga), renewal me domain renewal included hai.

## Refund

**Q: Order cancel karun to refund milega?**
A: Kaam shuru hone se pehle cancel karo to poora advance refund hoga. Kaam shuru hone ke baad advance refund nahi hoga. Detail `REFUND.md` me hai.
