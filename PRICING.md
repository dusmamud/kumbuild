## Yearly renewal — ₹2,999 / saal

Renewal me kya milega:

- Domain renewal
- Hosting
- Chhoti updates (phone number, timing, ek-do lines badalna)
- Support (WhatsApp se message)
- Saal me ek baar poori site ki health jaanch

Renewal na karne pe kya hoga: website ka **static hissa customer ka rehta hai** (handover, koi lock-in nahi), lekin dashboard, reminders aur enquiry list jaisi backend suvidhayein band ho jayengi. Domain customer ke naam par hai — chahe to domain/hosting khud sambhal le, Renewal lena zabardasti nahi.

## Payment terms

- **Advance:** 50% order ke time
- **Final:** baaki 50% approval + delivery pe, website live karne se pehle
- **Premium/Store:** 2–3 kishton me bhugtan ki suvidha
- **Revision:** Starter/Business me 2 rounds free, Premium me 3. Uske baad har extra round ka charge — amount TBD
- **Extra page:** package se zyada page chahiye to per-page rate (extra page ₹1,499, blog post ₹999)

## Chalta kharcha (Model A) — pricing page par bhi likha hai

Kuch cheezein ek baar banti hain (website, CMS, setup) — woh plan ke daam me hai. Teen cheezon ka kharcha har mahine teesri company ko jata hai, isliye account customer ke apne naam par banta hai aur bill seedha unhi ka aata hai:

- **Business email:** Zoho Mail free plan par ₹0 (5 users tak). Zyada suvidha: Zoho Lite ₹59/user/mahina ya Google Workspace ₹99/user/mahina se.
- **WhatsApp reminder:** Meta ka rate — utility ₹0.115/message (200 reminders ≈ ₹27/mahina); marketing message ₹0.8631/message. Alag platform fee nahi.
- **Email bhejna (form alerts):** shuruaat me hamare system se — chhote volume par Care/Renewal me sama jata hai.
- **Domain renewal:** dusre saal se Renewal (₹2,999/yr) me shamil.
- **Backend hosting (hamari VPS):** core plan me shamil; dusre saal se Renewal se chalta hai.

**Care vs Renewal:** Care kisi plan ke saath zaroori nahi hai — sirf tab lo jab mahine ke chhote changes, report aur pehle jawab chahiye. Renewal (₹2,999/saal) domain + hosting ka kharcha hai.

## Imaandari wali baat

Kuch waade peeche ke system se jude hain — enquiry list/dashboard ke liye form wala backend live hona baki hai, online payment ke liye Razorpay account, Calendar booking ke liye Google Calendar jodna. Pricing page par ye cheezein "live" tabhi likhi jayengi jab asal me chalu ho jayein — tab tak woh plan me shamil hain, live hone ka daava nahi.

## Kya price me nahi hai

- Customer ki photos / content banana — customer dega (stock photos shamil hain)
- Pehle saal ka domain alag se nahi — package price me hi hai (free domain pehla saal)
- Payment gateway ki fees — Razorpay standard 2% + 18% GST on fee, transaction se kat-ti hai (detail `PAYMENTS.md` me). Ye cost package price me se jayegi, customer se extra nahi liya jayega

## Price badalne ka rule

- Jo customer pehle se order kar chuka hai, uska price order ke time wala hi rahega
- Nayi pricing sirf naye orders pe lagegi
- Renewal price badle to customer ko renewal se pehle batana hoga
