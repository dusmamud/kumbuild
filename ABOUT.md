# About Kumbuild

> **Kumbuild — Tell us what to build.**

## Kumbuild kya hai?

Kumbuild ek done-for-you website service hai. Matlab customer ko website banana nahi aata, usko code nahi seekhna, aur usko kisi tool me ghanton design nahi karna. Customer sirf batata hai usko kya chahiye — Kumbuild website bana ke de deta hai.

Ye koi DIY website builder nahi hai. Ye ek service hai: tum batao, hum bana ke denge.

## Kumbuild kya karta hai?

Chhote business ke liye professional website banata hai, order lene se leke live karne tak:

1. **Business website** — business kya karta hai, services, price, photos, location, contact. Shop, clinic, salon, gym, restaurant ke liye.
2. **Landing page** — Instagram / Facebook page ke liye ek proper page. Bio link se aane wala visitor yahan business ko samajh ke WhatsApp ya call pe enquiry kar sakta hai.
3. **Booking / enquiry website** — clinic, salon, tuition, coaching ke liye appointment ya enquiry form. Customer form bhare, business ko WhatsApp / email pe enquiry mile.
4. **Catalogue / menu website** — shop ya restaurant ke liye products ya menu list, photo aur price ke saath, aur WhatsApp order button.

### Har website me kya milega?

- Mobile pe perfect dikhegi — zyada customers phone se hi aayenge
- Fast loading — halki website, bina bhaari animation ke
- WhatsApp button — customer ek tap me chat kar sake
- Google Maps — dukaan / clinic / centre kahan hai, rasta mile
- Contact / enquiry form — naam, phone, message bhej sake
- Basic SEO — Google pe business ka naam aur services dhoondhne pe website milne ke liye basic setup

### Kumbuild kya nahi karta hai? (Scope — abhi ke liye)

- Mobile app nahi banata (Android / iOS app is service ka hissa nahi hai)
- Bada e-commerce store / marketplace nahi banata (payment gateway wala full online shop alag kaam hai)
- Logo design ya poora branding package nahi deta (customer ka naam, photos aur content hi use hoga)
- Social media manage nahi karta (Instagram / Facebook page chalana customer ka kaam hai, website sirf us page ko ek proper jagah deti hai)

Ye scope abhi ka hai. Baad me badhana ho to alag decision hoga.

## Target audience — kiske liye hai?

### 1. Dukaan / shop owner

Kirana shop, mobile shop, electronics, kapde ki dukaan, bakery. Problem: aas-paas ke log Google pe "mere paas mobile shop" dhoondhte hain, par dukaan online kahin nahi milti. Chahiye: ek page jahan dukaan ka naam, kya milta hai, timing, location aur WhatsApp number ho.

### 2. Clinic / doctor / health service

Clinic, dental clinic, physiotherapy, lab. Problem: patient ko timing, services, fees aur location poochhne ke liye baar-baar call karna padta hai. Chahiye: services + timing + appointment / enquiry form wali website.

### 3. Tuition / coaching / classes

Tuition centre, coaching, dance, yoga, music classes. Problem: parents ko fees, batches, subjects aur admission process ke liye alag-alag jagah poochhna padta hai. Chahiye: courses, fees, batch timing aur enquiry form wali website.

### 4. Salon / gym / restaurant / cafe

Services aur price list, photos, timing, location aur booking / order enquiry — ek jagah pe.

### 5. Instagram / Facebook page wale seller aur creator

Boutique, home baker, photographer, freelancer, handmade products. Problem: Instagram page hai, par wahan se trust nahi banta aur order lene ka koi proper tareeka nahi hai. Chahiye: ek professional page jo Instagram bio link se khule, jahan kaam, price aur WhatsApp order ho.

### Kiske liye nahi hai?

- Badi company jisko custom software, CRM ya bada portal chahiye
- Jisko khud website builder me baith ke design karna hai (woh DIY user hai, Kumbuild ka customer nahi hai)
- Jisko sirf ek free page chahiye aur kuch pay nahi karna (Kumbuild paid service hai)

## Kaise kaam karta hai? (Customer ke liye, 5 steps)

1. **Batao** — kumbuild.com pe package choose karo aur form bharo: business ka naam, kya karte ho, services, WhatsApp number, photos, location.
2. **Advance do** — package ka 50% advance payment karo.
3. **Preview dekho** — 3–5 din (draft) me website ka preview link milega.
4. **Approve karo** — changes batao (2 free revision rounds, draft), phir baaki 50% payment karo.
5. **Live** — website live ho jayegi, aur har saal renewal pe domain + hosting + chhoti updates milengi.

## Kumbuild kyun alag hai?

- **DIY builder se alag:** Wix / WordPress me customer ko khud banana padta hai. Kumbuild me customer sirf batata hai, banana Kumbuild ka kaam hai.
- **Agency se alag:** Agency mehngi hoti hai aur hafton lagati hai. Kumbuild chhote business ke liye sasta, fast (3–5 din, draft) aur ek fixed package me hai.
- **WhatsApp-first:** India ke chhote business ka customer WhatsApp pe baat karta hai. Isliye har website me WhatsApp button aur enquiry WhatsApp pe aana core feature hai, add-on nahi.
- **Phone-first:** Customer phone se dekhega, isliye mobile pe perfect hona pehli shart hai, desktop baad me.
