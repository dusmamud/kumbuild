When someone nearby searches for what you do, your website should be the result they find. Every Kumbuild website launches with the technical side done:

## What we set up for you

- **Titles and descriptions** written for each page, with your service and your area.
- **Google Maps listing guidance** — your business on the map, linked to your website.
- **A sitemap submitted to Google**, so every page gets found and indexed.
- **Fast, mobile-first pages** — most of your customers are on phones, often on slow networks, and Google ranks fast pages higher.

## What you can do

Two things help more than everything else combined:

1. **Ask happy customers for Google reviews**, and reply to them. A steady trickle of real reviews is the strongest local signal there is.
2. **Keep your timings and phone number current** — on the website (message us, see [Changes after launch](/your-website/changes-after-launch/)) and on your Maps listing. Wrong timings lose visits silently.

## Writing that brings customers

Short, practical articles — what things cost, how to choose, what to expect — bring searchers to you before they are ready to buy.
