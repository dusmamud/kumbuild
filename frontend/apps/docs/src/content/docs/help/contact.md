## WhatsApp — the fastest way

Message us on WhatsApp and a person who knows your website will answer — not a support queue, not a ticket number. Use it for:

- Questions before ordering
- Changes to your live website
- Anything urgent about your site

[Chat on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/919876543210)

## Email

Prefer email? Write to [hello@kumbuild.com](mailto:hello@kumbuild.com). WhatsApp is faster, but every email gets an answer.
