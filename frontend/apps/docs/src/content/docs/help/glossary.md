Technical words, explained the way we would explain them in person. If a guide uses a word that is not here, message us on WhatsApp and we will add it.

## Analytics

A simple count of how many people visit your website, which pages they look at, and where they came from. It tells you what customers are interested in — not who they are.

## Domain

Your website's address — the name customers type to reach you, for example `kumbuild.com`. You own it, and it is yours to keep even if you leave Kumbuild.

## Hosting

The computer, somewhere on the internet, where your website's files live so anyone can open them at any time. Kumbuild handles hosting for you; it is covered by the yearly renewal.

## Mobile-first

Design that starts with the phone screen and then adapts to larger screens — because most of your customers visit on a phone. The opposite approach (desktop first, squeezed onto a phone) is why so many sites are hard to use on mobile.

## Preview link

A private web address where you can see your website before it goes live. Only people with the link can open it. See [Preview and revisions](/your-website/preview-and-revisions/).

## Renewal

The yearly payment — ₹1,999/year after your first year — that keeps your hosting, domain, security updates and backups running. See [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/).

## Responsive

A website that rearranges itself to fit whatever screen opens it — phone, tablet, or computer — instead of looking broken on small screens.

## Revision round

One collected list of changes you ask for after seeing your preview. Two revision rounds are free with every package — send your changes together in one message and your preview gets updated faster. See [Preview and revisions](/your-website/preview-and-revisions/).

## SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

Everything that helps your website show up when someone searches on Google — good page titles, fast pages, a sitemap, and real reviews from customers. See [Get found on Google](/grow/get-found-on-google/).

## Sitemap

A simple list of all the pages on your website, given to Google so it can find and list every page. We create and submit it for you when your site launches.

## SSL / HTTPS

The padlock in the browser address bar. It means the connection between your customer and your website is encrypted, so nobody in between can read or change it. Every Kumbuild website has it.

## WhatsApp button

A button on your website that opens a WhatsApp chat with your business number in one tap — so a customer can ask a question or place an order without typing your number.
