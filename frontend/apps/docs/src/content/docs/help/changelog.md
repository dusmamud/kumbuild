What has changed on this docs site, newest first. This page covers the docs themselves — changes to your website are handled on WhatsApp, not here.

## Unreleased

- **Ten new guides added.** Quickstart, What you get, Before you order, Content guide, Launch checklist, Managing your site, Troubleshooting, Glossary, Your first customers online — and this changelog. The sidebar now has four groups: Start here, Your website, Grow, and Help. Written 21 November 2026; **not yet published** — this batch is still uncommitted, so treat this entry as the current work in progress, not a release.

## 14 November 2026

- **Maintenance and renewal page linked from the docs.** The [payments and renewal guide](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/) now points to the maintenance page, which lists everything the ₹1,999/year renewal covers.

## 13 November 2026

- **Dark theme applied to the docs.** The site now follows the same dark theme as the rest of Kumbuild, with a light/dark toggle in the header.

## 11 November 2026

- **Docs site launched.** The first nine guides went live: How Kumbuild works, Order your website, Get found on Google, Contact and support, Frequently asked questions, Changes after launch, Preview and revisions, and Payments and renewal — plus this home page.

:::note[About these dates]
Every date above comes from the Kumbuild repo history and can be checked there. If a change is missing from this list, it was too small to note — the guides themselves are always the current version.
:::
