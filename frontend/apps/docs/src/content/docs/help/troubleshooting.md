## The preview link does not open

**What you see:** tapping the link does nothing, or the page stays blank, or it says the page cannot be found.

**Likely cause:** the link was typed instead of tapped (long preview links break easily when retyped), or you are on a weak connection, or the link expired after the preview was updated.

**Fix:**

1. Tap the link directly in WhatsApp — do not copy it by hand.
2. If it still does not open, switch from mobile data to Wi-Fi (or the other way round) and try again.
3. Close the browser tab completely and tap the link once more.

**Contact us if:** the link still fails after these steps. Tell us what your screen shows — a screenshot helps — and we will send a fresh link.

## My site is not showing on Google yet

**What you see:** you search for your business name and your website does not appear, or only your Maps listing shows.

**Likely cause:** this is normal for a new site. Google needs days — sometimes a couple of weeks — to find and list a new website, even when the sitemap has been submitted. It is not a sign that anything is broken.

**Fix:**

1. Wait a few days after launch before worrying.
2. Search for your exact business name in quotes, e.g. `"Sharma Hair Salon Gaya"` — a new site shows up for its own name before it shows up for general searches like "hair salon near me".
3. Check your Google Maps listing is live and linked to your website — that is what most nearby customers see first.

**Contact us if:** your site still does not appear for its exact business name after two weeks. See also [Get found on Google](/grow/get-found-on-google/).

## The WhatsApp button or contact form is not working

**What you see:** tapping the WhatsApp button does nothing, opens a chat with the wrong number, or a customer says they messaged you and you never got it.

**Likely cause:** the number on the site is wrong or has changed, or the customer messaged a different number.

**Fix:**

1. Tap the button yourself and check it opens a chat with your number.
2. If the number is wrong, message us on WhatsApp with the correct one — it is a small change, fixed quickly. See [Changes after launch](/your-website/changes-after-launch/).
3. If customers say they messaged you, ask them to show you the chat — it often turns out they messaged an old number from a visiting card or an old listing.

**Contact us if:** the button opens the right chat for you but customers still cannot reach you — describe exactly what they see.

## Photos look wrong

**What you see:** a photo is blurry, stretched, sideways, or shows the wrong thing.

**Likely cause:** the photo was sent as a screenshot, or WhatsApp compressed it, or it was taken in poor light.

**Fix:**

1. Check the photo on your own phone first — if it looks bad there, it will look bad on the site.
2. Re-send the original photo from your gallery as a **document** on WhatsApp (attach → document → pick the photo), which keeps full quality.
3. Retake it in daylight with a tidy background if the original is dark or blurry.

**Contact us if:** the original photo looks fine on your phone but wrong on the site — send us the original and tell us which page it is on. Tips for good photos: [Content guide](/your-website/content-guide/).

## A payment or renewal question

**What you see:** you are unsure whether a payment went through, or you got a renewal reminder and are not sure what it covers.

**Likely cause:** payment links and reminders all come through WhatsApp from us, so anything that does not come from our WhatsApp thread deserves a second look.

**Fix:**

1. Check the payment status in your own UPI or banking app first.
2. The renewal is ₹1,999/year after your first year and covers hosting, your domain, security updates and backups. Full details: [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/).
3. Never pay a renewal link that did not come from our WhatsApp conversation — if you are unsure, ask us before paying.

**Contact us if:** a payment is not showing as received, or you want to confirm a renewal date or amount.
