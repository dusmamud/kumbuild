## What is Kumbuild?

Kumbuild is a done-for-you website service. You tell us what to build, and we build the website for you. You don't need to learn code or design anything in a tool.

## How many days will my website take?

3–5 working days after advance payment and complete content — until you get the preview link. If content comes late, the time moves ahead.

## What do I need to give you?

Business name, services and prices, WhatsApp number, address, timings, and real photos. The full list is in [Order your website](/getting-started/order-your-website/).

## How much will a website cost?

Starter ₹3,999, Business ₹7,999, Premium ₹14,999. Yearly renewal is ₹1,999. See [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/) for how the two payments work.

## How do I pay?

50% advance at order time, the remaining 50% after approval, before the website goes live. Payment is through Razorpay — UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets.

## What if I don't like the website?

Two revision rounds are free. See the preview, tell us the changes, and we will send a new preview. How rounds work is explained in [Preview and revisions](/your-website/preview-and-revisions/).
