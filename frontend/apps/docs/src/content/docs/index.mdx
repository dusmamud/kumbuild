## How help works here

Three promises, so you always know where you stand:

- **A person answers.** Support is WhatsApp with the team that built your site — no ticket queue, no bot maze.
- **Plain words only.** If a guide needs a technical word, it explains it in the same line.
- **Your site, your property.** Domain, content and data are yours; leaving never traps you. See [what you own](/getting-started/how-kumbuild-works/#what-you-own).
