Your website is only as good as the content on it. You do not need professional writing or a camera — just real words and real photos, prepared the right way.

## Your text

- **Write like you talk to a customer.** "We repair all brands of washing machines at your home, same day in most areas" beats a paragraph of formal language.
- **Keep it short.** One or two lines per service is enough. Customers scan; they do not read essays.
- **Prices must be current.** A wrong price on the site is worse than no price — check every number before you send it, and tell us on WhatsApp the moment one changes.
- **Timings and address, exactly.** Copy them from your shop board or Google Maps listing so all three match.

## Your photos

Photos are the single biggest difference between a website that looks real and one that looks empty. A few rules:

- **Send originals, not screenshots.** Screenshots are smaller, blurrier versions of the real photo — send the photo itself from your gallery.
- **Good light beats a good camera.** Daylight, or your shop lights on full. Avoid dark, blurry, or tilted shots.
- **Show the real thing.** Your shop front, your products, your work in progress, your team. Stock photos from the internet make the site look generic — and customers notice.
- **Aim for 5–10 photos** to start: shop front, inside the shop, 3–4 products or services, and your team if you like.
- **Tidy the frame.** Move the clutter out of shot, wipe the lens, and take one step back so the subject has breathing room.

| Format | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| JPG or PNG from your phone | Photos — send as they are, we handle sizing |
| PNG with transparent background | Your logo, if you have one |
| PDF or a photo of a printed menu/price list | Only if you have no digital copy — we will type it out |

:::tip[WhatsApp compresses photos]
If you send photos on WhatsApp, send them as **documents** (attach → document → pick the photo) instead of as images. That keeps the full quality.
:::

## Your logo

- Send the original file, not a photo of a signboard.
- PNG with a transparent background is best; JPG is fine if that is all you have.
- No logo? We use your business name in clean type — it looks good, and you can add a logo later.

## What makes good content

- **Real beats perfect.** A slightly imperfect photo of your actual shop builds more trust than a perfect photo of someone else's.
- **Specific beats vague.** "Home delivery in 45 minutes within 5 km" is useful; "fast delivery" is not.
- **Current beats complete.** Send what you have now and add the rest later — waiting for the perfect set of photos is the most common reason launches slip.
