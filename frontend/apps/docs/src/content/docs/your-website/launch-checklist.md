Before your website goes live, we check everything on this list. Use it yourself when you review your preview, too — you know your business better than anyone, and a second pair of eyes catches what we cannot.

## Business details

- [ ] Business name spelled exactly right, everywhere
- [ ] Phone number correct — tap it on your phone and check it dials
- [ ] WhatsApp number correct — tap the WhatsApp button and check it opens a chat with you
- [ ] Address correct, matching your Google Maps listing
- [ ] Opening hours correct, including holidays if you close on any

## Prices and services

- [ ] Every service listed is one you actually offer
- [ ] Every price is current — no old rates, no missing items
- [ ] No placeholder text anywhere ("lorem ipsum", "coming soon", sample prices)

## Photos

- [ ] All photos are yours, in the right places
- [ ] No blurry, sideways, or stretched photos
- [ ] No screenshots used where the original photo exists

## Links and buttons

- [ ] Every button goes somewhere — no dead links
- [ ] WhatsApp button opens a chat with your number
- [ ] Phone links dial your number
- [ ] Social links (if any) go to your real pages

## On your phone

- [ ] Open the preview on your phone — that is how most customers will see it
- [ ] Nothing overlaps, nothing runs off the screen
- [ ] Text is readable without zooming

:::tip[Check it like a customer]
Pretend you have never seen your business. Can you find what you sell, what it costs, and how to reach you — within a few seconds? If not, tell us on WhatsApp; that is exactly what a revision round is for.
:::

## What happens at launch

When you approve the preview and the balance is paid, the site goes live on your domain — usually the same day. We then submit the sitemap to Google so your pages start getting found; new sites take some days to appear in search, which is normal. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) if anything looks off after launch.
