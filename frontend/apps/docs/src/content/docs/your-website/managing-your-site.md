Your website is built and hosted by us, so there is no dashboard you need to learn and no settings you can accidentally break. That is the point — but it helps to know where the line sits.

## What to ask us for

Almost everything on your site goes through us, by WhatsApp message. That is the normal way, not a limitation:

- **Prices, timings, phone number, address** — message us the new value and the page it is on.
- **Photos** — send new ones as originals (or as documents on WhatsApp, so they keep full quality).
- **New pages** — a festival offer, a new service, a gallery. This is a small project: we quote a fair price before starting, and the new page goes through the same preview-and-approval flow.
- **Anything that looks broken** — a button that does nothing, a photo that will not load, a page that looks wrong on your phone. Tell us what you see; see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) first for the quick checks.

Full details: [Changes after launch](/your-website/changes-after-launch/).

## What you can do yourself

These do not need us at all:

- **Your Google Maps listing** — opening hours, photos, replying to reviews. Keep it matching your website; mismatched timings lose customers silently. See [Get found on Google](/grow/get-found-on-google/).
- **Asking customers for reviews** — a WhatsApp message after a happy visit ("we're on Google Maps, a review would help a lot") is the strongest local signal there is.
- **Sharing your site** — send your website link on WhatsApp, put it in your Instagram bio, print it on a bill or visiting card. See [Your first customers online](/grow/your-first-customers-online/).

## What you never need to worry about

Hosting, security updates, backups, and keeping the site fast — all handled by us, covered by the ₹1,999/year renewal after your first year. We remind you on WhatsApp before the renewal date; nothing is charged silently. Details in [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/).

:::note[Want to leave?]
Your domain, content and customer data are yours. If you ever move away, we hand over the files and help with the move — leaving never traps you.
:::
