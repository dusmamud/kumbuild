---
title: Changes after launch
description: How to get prices, photos and pages changed once your website is live.
---

Your business changes — prices move, new products arrive, timings shift. Your website should keep up.

## Small changes

Message us on WhatsApp with what needs changing: a price, a photo, a phone number, opening hours. Small text and photo changes are turned around quickly, and you do not need to touch any dashboard or editor.

## Bigger changes

Want a new page — a festival offer, a new service, a gallery? That is a small project: tell us what you have in mind on WhatsApp, we will quote a fair price before starting, and the new page goes through the same [preview and approval](/your-website/preview-and-revisions/) flow as your original website.

## What to send us

- The page and the exact words or prices to change
- New photos as phone photos — originals, not screenshots, look best

That is all. We make the change, send you the updated link to check, and it goes live on your approval.
