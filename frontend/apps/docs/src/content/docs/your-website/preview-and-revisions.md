Before anyone in the world can see your website, you see it first — on a private preview link we send to your WhatsApp.

## Checking your preview

Open the link on your phone (that is how your customers will see it) and look at:

- Your business name, phone number and WhatsApp number — are they right?
- Prices, timings and addresses.
- The photos — yours, in the right places.

## Asking for changes

Just message us on WhatsApp in your own words — "change the banner photo", "the price of the haircut is wrong", "make the button bigger". Two full revision rounds are included free with every package.

A **revision round** means one collected list of changes. It helps to send your changes together in one message rather than one-by-one — your preview gets updated faster.

## Approving

When the preview looks right, tell us "approved" on WhatsApp. We then take the balance payment and put the website live — usually the same day. Details in [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/).
