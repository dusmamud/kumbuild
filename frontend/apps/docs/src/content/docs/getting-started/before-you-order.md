title: Before you order
description: What to have ready before you fill the order form — business details, photos, services and prices.
---

Ordering takes two minutes, and you can order before everything is ready. But the preview clock only starts when we have your advance payment *and* your complete content — so gathering these five things first is the fastest way to go live in 3–5 working days.

## 1. Your business details

- Business name, exactly as customers know it
- What you sell or do, in one or two lines
- City and address
- Phone number and WhatsApp number
- Opening hours

## 2. Your services and prices

A simple list is enough — for example, "Haircut ₹200, Beard trim ₹100". Send it as text on WhatsApp or in the order form; it does not need to be a formatted document. If a price changes later, that is a small change — see [Changes after launch](/your-website/changes-after-launch/).

## 3. Your photos

Real photos of your shop, your products, your work, your team — whatever you have on your phone is fine. Good light and a tidy background matter more than a fancy camera. Send originals, not screenshots. How many and what to shoot: see the [Content guide](/your-website/content-guide/).

:::note[No photos yet?]
Order anyway. We can start with the design and add your photos when you take them — most owners send them over WhatsApp over a day or two.
:::

## 4. Your logo, if you have one

Send the original logo file if you have it — a PNG with a transparent background is best. No logo? No problem: we use your business name in clean type. We do not design logos.

## 5. Your domain, if you already own one

If you already bought a domain name somewhere else, tell us — we will help point it at your new website. If you do not have one, we handle the domain as part of your package; you do not need to buy anything yourself.

## A quick checklist

- [ ] Business name, city, address, timings
- [ ] Phone and WhatsApp number
- [ ] Services and prices as a simple list
- [ ] 5–10 real photos on your phone
- [ ] Logo file, if you have one
- [ ] Domain details, only if you already own one
