Ordering takes about two minutes, on the [order form](https://kumbuild.com/order). Nothing is charged at this step.

## What the form asks

- **Your business** — name, what you sell or do, and your city.
- **The package** — Starter, Business or Premium. Not sure? Pick the closest one; we will confirm the right fit on WhatsApp before any payment.
- **Your photos** — shop, products, menu, team — whatever you have on your phone is fine.
- **Your WhatsApp number** — this is how we talk: confirmations, the preview link, and support all happen there.

## What happens next

1. We message you on WhatsApp to confirm the details.
2. You pay the 50% advance through a Razorpay link (UPI, cards and net-banking all work).
3. Your preview is ready in 3–5 working days — see [Preview and revisions](/your-website/preview-and-revisions/).

:::note
No photos yet? Order anyway. We can start with the design and add your photos when you take them — most owners send them over WhatsApp over a day or two.
:::
