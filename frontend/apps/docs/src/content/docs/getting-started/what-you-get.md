Every Kumbuild website is built for you — you never open a builder, write code, or figure out hosting. Here is what that covers, and where it stops.

## Included in every package

- **A website built around your business** — your name, your services, your prices, your photos, your contact details. Real pages, not a template with your name pasted in.
- **Mobile-first design** — most of your customers will visit on a phone, often on a slow connection, so the site is designed for small screens first and made to load fast.
- **Contact and WhatsApp** — your phone number and a WhatsApp button, so a customer can reach you in one tap.
- **Google setup** — page titles and descriptions written for your service and your area, a sitemap submitted to Google, and guidance for your Google Maps listing. Details in [Get found on Google](/grow/get-found-on-google/).
- **A private preview before launch** — you see the whole site on a preview link and approve it before anyone else can. Two revision rounds are free. See [Preview and revisions](/your-website/preview-and-revisions/).
- **Your domain, hosting and security** — the first year is included in your package price; after that the ₹1,999/year renewal keeps everything running. See [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/).
- **Your content stays yours** — domain, content and customer data belong to you. If you ever move away, we hand over the files and help with the move.

## What is NOT included

- **Writing your content from nothing** — we polish and lay out what you send, but the words, prices and photos have to come from you. Our [Content guide](/your-website/content-guide/) shows what good content looks like.
- **A logo or new photos** — if you have a logo, send it; if not, we work with your business name in clean type. We do not shoot photos or design a logo for you.
- **Running your marketing** — we set up the Google basics, but asking customers for reviews, posting offers, and keeping your Maps listing current are yours. Start with [Your first customers online](/grow/your-first-customers-online/).
- **Unlimited changes after launch** — small text and photo changes are quick and easy (see [Changes after launch](/your-website/changes-after-launch/)), but a brand-new page is a small separate project, quoted before we start.
