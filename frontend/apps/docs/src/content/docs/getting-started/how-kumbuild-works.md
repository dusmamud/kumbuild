Kumbuild is a done-for-you website service. You never open a website builder, write code, or figure out hosting — you tell us about your business, approve what we build, and go live.

## The five steps

1. **Tell us.** Fill the [order form](https://kumbuild.com/order) — your business details, the package you want, your photos, and your WhatsApp number. Two minutes, no payment yet.
2. **Advance.** We confirm the details with you on WhatsApp and share a Razorpay payment link for the 50% advance. Work starts when the advance arrives.
3. **Preview.** Within 3–5 working days you get a private preview link of your website — real pages, your photos, your prices.
4. **Approve.** You check the preview and can ask for changes — two revision rounds are free. Tell us on WhatsApp, in your own words; no forms.
5. **Go live.** Happy with the preview? Pay the remaining 50% and your website goes live on your domain, set up so customers find you on Google.

## What you own

Your domain, your content and your customer data are yours. If you ever move away from Kumbuild, everything goes with you — we will hand over the files and help with the move.

## What happens after launch

Your website keeps running — hosting, security updates and the yearly renewal are handled by us (see [Payments and renewal](/your-website/payments-and-renewal/)). Small changes after launch follow [Changes after launch](/your-website/changes-after-launch/).
