Open Google and search for your own business the way a stranger would — your service plus your area. Whatever appears is your shop window for most new customers, whether you built it or not.

For many small businesses that window is a half-empty listing, an old phone number, and a competitor right below with photos, timings and a website. The fix is not complicated: correct details everywhere, real photos, and one page of your own that answers the questions customers actually ask.

A website you own is the one result nobody can edit but you. Prices, timings, photos, offers — current, because keeping them current is part of the job you hand to us.
