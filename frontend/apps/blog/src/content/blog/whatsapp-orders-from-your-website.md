Watch how your customers already buy: they ask on WhatsApp, you confirm, they pay when they collect or by UPI. The conversation is the checkout — it is personal, and it works.

So a small business website should not copy a big e-commerce store. It should show everything clearly — photos, names, prices — and put a WhatsApp button on every item, opening a chat with the order already written.

That is how every Kumbuild catalogue is built. No commission, no new habits for your customers, and you keep the relationship that makes them come back.
