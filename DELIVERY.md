# Kumbuild — Delivery Policy

Status: **DRAFT (2026-11-09). Ye policy website pe daalne se pehle user ko final karni hai.**

## Delivery time — DRAFT

- **3–5 working days** — advance payment + poora content milne ke baad, preview link milne tak.
- Working days ka matlab: Monday–Saturday (Sunday chhutti mana gaya hai — ye assumption hai, final karte time confirm karna hai).
- Agar customer content late dega to delivery time usi hisaab se aage badhega. Clock tabhi chalega jab sab kuch mil gaya ho.

## Delivery stages

| Stage | Matlab | Customer ko kaise pata chalega |
|---|---|---|
| Received | Order aur advance mil gaya (admin ne admin dashboard me order add kiya) | Admin WhatsApp pe batayega: order mil gaya |
| In progress | Website ban rahi hai | Admin batayega jab kaam shuru ho |
| Preview ready | Preview link bhej diya | Preview link WhatsApp pe milega — kholo, dekho |
| Revision | Customer ne changes bole | WhatsApp pe changes batao |
| Approved | Customer ne approve kiya, final payment ho gaya | WhatsApp pe confirm hoga |
| Live | Website live hai | Live link WhatsApp pe milega |

## Revision policy — DRAFT

- **2 revision rounds free** hain.
- Ek round ka matlab: customer ek baar me saare changes bata dega, Kumbuild unhe karke dobara preview bhejega.
- 2 rounds ke baad har extra round ka charge lagega — amount TBD.
- Revision ka matlab: text badalna, photo badalna, color / layout me chhoti changes. Poori website dobara design karna revision nahi hai — woh naya order hoga.

## Customer ko kya dena hoga (content)

Delivery tabhi shuru hogi jab ye sab mil jayega. Detail `CONTENT.md` me hai.

- Business ka naam, kya karte ho, services aur price
- WhatsApp number, phone, email, address, timing
- Real photos (dukaan / clinic / products / kaam ki)
- Logo (agar hai to)

Kuch missing ho to Kumbuild customer ko message karega aur order ruk jayega — delivery time us din se nahi gina jayega jis din content adhoora tha.

## Approval aur go-live

1. Preview link milega (WhatsApp pe) — customer dekhega, changes batayega ya approve karega
2. Approve karne ke baad final 50% payment karna hoga
3. Final payment milne ke baad website live ki jayegi
4. Live hone ke baad customer ko live link WhatsApp pe milega

## Delivery ke baad

- **Chhoti updates** renewal me included hain (phone, timing, ek-do lines — limit TBD)
- **Bade changes** (naya page, design badalna) alag charge honge — amount TBD
- **Support:** WhatsApp se message kar sakte ho — response time TBD (koi waada abhi nahi kiya gaya hai)

## Agar customer jawab na de

Preview bhejne ke baad customer 7 din (draft) tak jawab na de to Kumbuild ek reminder bhejega. Uske baad bhi jawab na aaye to order `On hold` me chala jayega aur delivery time dobara shuru hoga jab customer jawab dega. Ye number (7 din) draft hai — final karte time confirm karna hai.
